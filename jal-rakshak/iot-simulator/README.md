# IoT Simulator — JAL-RAKSHAK

Simulates real-world smart water distribution networks:
- **Sensors**: Flow meters, pipe pressure transducers, water level sensors, water quality probes (pH, turbidity, dissolved solids).
- **MQTT**: Publishes telemetry topics to backend or broker.
- **Simulation**: Generates baseline conditions, sudden pipe bursts, valve failures, and water contamination events.
